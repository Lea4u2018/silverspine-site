ABOUT FACEBOOK REACH (plain talk)
════════════════════════════════════════════════
You’re not imagining the quiet.

What often happens with personal FB (especially after 2020–2024):
• The Feed now pushes Reels / video more than still photo posts.
• If you were quiet for a year, the algorithm “forgot” you — restart is slow.
• Inspirational text-only posts get fewer forced views than short video.
• Pages and Profiles both got less free reach industry-wide; 1,000 followers
  no longer means 1,000 people see each post. Often it’s a small slice.

What helps (without changing who you are):
1) Post a SHORT video (9–20 sec) more than plain text.
2) Reply to every early comment in the first hour.
3) Ask one gentle question at the end (people answer → reach widens).
4) Cross-post the SAME day to IG + point them to silverspinestudio.com
5) Don’t chase controversy — your clean, faith-forward voice is a feature.
6) Use your personal profile for “human Leameso,” Studio page for book ads.

You’re not failing. The platform changed. This track rebuilds visibility with
your voice + different clips.
